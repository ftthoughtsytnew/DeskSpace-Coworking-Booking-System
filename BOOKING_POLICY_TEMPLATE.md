# Booking Policy – Draft Template

Customize all amounts, hours and timelines before publishing.

- Booking durations: hourly, daily and monthly.
- A booking is confirmed when the system successfully creates a reservation.
- Online payment bookings must be marked paid only after verified payment confirmation from the payment provider.
- Pay-at-office bookings remain payment-pending until staff records payment.
- Cancellation, rescheduling, late arrival, no-show and refund rules must be clearly stated here.
- Workspace opening hours, check-in process and guest rules must be added before launch.
